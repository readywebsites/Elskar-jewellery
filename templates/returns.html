Returns & Refunds Policy

Our 15-Day Luxury Guarantee & Seamless Replacements

Every piece of Azure Jewels fine jewellery is individually inspected by our certified gemmologists prior to dispatch. If for any reason your selected creation does not meet your expectations, we offer a straightforward, insured 15-day return and exchange service.

15-Day Return Window

Initiate returns within 15 calendar days from the date of confirmed courier delivery.

Insured Reverse Pickup

Complimentary armored courier pickup arranged directly from your doorstep.

Direct Bank Refund

Full refund to your original payment mode within 5–7 business days post-inspection.

1. Return Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a full refund or exchange under our 15-Day Luxury Guarantee, items must satisfy the following conditions:

  • The jewellery must be unworn, in pristine, unused condition, without scratches, dents, or signs of wear.
  • The original tamper-evident security seal tag must remain unbroken and firmly attached to the piece.
  • All accompanying certificates of authenticity, hallmarking documentation, and warranty cards must be returned intact.
  • The item must be returned in the original velvet packaging box, velvet pouch, and protective rigid carton.

2. Return Window & Deadlines

Return requests must be submitted within 15 calendar days of parcel receipt as confirmed by the courier tracking log. Requests submitted after 15 days cannot be processed for return or refund, but remain eligible for lifetime maintenance and replating services under our Atelier Care policy.

3. Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items

The following categories are strictly non-returnable due to bespoke manufacturing and hygiene standards:

  • Customized & Engraved Creations: Pieces personalized with initials, custom sizing modifications, or bespoke bridal suite commissions.
  • Pierced Earrings with Broken Hygiene Seals: Items whose hygienic protective wrappers have been compromised.
  • Archive Final Clearance Items: Pieces marked as "Final Sale" on promotional vaults.

4. How to Request a Return

  1. Contact Concierge: Email concierge@azurejewels.com or WhatsApp +91 98765 43210 with your Order Number (e.g. AZ-12345678) and reason for return.
  2. Verification: Our team reviews your request within 24 hours and issues a Return Authorization (RA) number along with insured shipping label documents.
  3. Secure Packing: Place the jewellery into its original velvet case with all certificates. Place inside the tamper-proof outer carton provided.
  4. Doorstep Pickup: Our designated armored logistics specialist will arrive with a tamper-evident seal pouch to collect the parcel.

5. Refund Processing & Timelines

Upon receipt at our central atelier in Mumbai, our quality appraisal team inspects the piece within 48 hours. Once approved:

  • Prepaid Orders (Cards/Net Banking/UPI): Refunds are credited back to your original source account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery Orders: Refunds are transferred via NEFT/IMPS to your verified bank account upon confirmation of your IFSC details.

6. Exchange Policy

If you wish to exchange a piece for a different ring size, chain length, or another design from our collections, we facilitate a one-time complimentary exchange. Any price differential will be charged or credited accordingly via store voucher or payment link.

7. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Deliveries

All parcels are fully insured during transit. In the rare event that your package arrives visibly tampered or the creation contains a manufacturing flaw:

  • Notify our concierge within 48 hours of delivery with clear unboxing photographs or video.
  • We will arrange an immediate priority pickup and dispatch an identical replacement or issue an unconditional 100% refund immediately.
