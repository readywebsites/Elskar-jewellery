ORDERS

Placing an order at Azure Jewels is simple and secure. Browse our collections or category galleries, select your desired piece, choose any available size options, and click "ADD TO CART" or "BUY NOW" for expedited checkout. When ready, navigate to your Shopping Bag, review your items, and follow the checkout prompts to provide your delivery address and preferred secure payment method.

To request cancellation, contact our concierge immediately via WhatsApp (+91 98765 43210) or email concierge@azurejewels.com with your order number. Bespoke commissions cannot be cancelled once atelier crafting has commenced.

If your order has not yet departed our atelier, our concierge will gladly assist in updating your shipping address, ring sizing preference, or gift note. Please contact our team within 2 to 3 hours of order submission for immediate assistance.

PAYMENTS

We support a comprehensive suite of secure payment options:
  • Major Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, RuPay).
  • UPI Payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and all banking UPI handles).
  • Net Banking across 50+ major Indian banks.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible domestic pin codes up to ₹25,000.

Uncompromisingly secure. All financial transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology and routed directly through certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway partners. Azure Jewels never stores or retains your payment card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials on our servers.

SHIPPING

Metropolitan centers (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) typically receive deliveries within 2 to 3 business days via express air courier. Tier-1 and Tier-2 destinations require 3 to 5 business days. Bespoke and made-to-order creations involve additional artisan crafting time as specified on the product page.

Once dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email notification with your Air Waybill (AWB) number and live tracking link. Authenticated patrons can also view order status in real time anytime by navigating to My Orders in your account dashboard.

RETURNS

We provide a 15-day luxury return guarantee on standard catalog pieces. Pieces must be unworn, in original velvet casing, with intact security seal tags and authenticity certificates. Custom engraved creations, altered sizes, and bespoke suites are non-returnable. Full details are available in our Returns & Refunds Policy.

Simply email concierge@azurejewels.com or message our WhatsApp helpline with your order number and request. Our concierge will schedule a complimentary insured reverse pickup from your residence.

PRODUCTS & CARE

100% certified authentic. Every creation crafted at Azure Jewels is accompanied by an official certificate of authenticity detailing metal purity, hallmarking registration, uncut polki specifications, and gemstone credentials. We stand behind every heirloom for a lifetime.

To preserve the luster and prevent tarnishing:
  • Store each piece separately in its velvet Azure pouch away from humidity.
  • Avoid direct contact with perfumes, hairsprays, sanitizers, and chlorine water.
  • Wipe gently after wear with the complimentary microfiber polishing cloth provided with your order.
  • Avail of complimentary ultrasonic cleaning and repolishing at our flagship atelier anytime.

Our jewellery specialists and bridal stylists are available daily to answer questions or arrange private showroom viewings.
